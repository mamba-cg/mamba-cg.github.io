How I work

Useful tools start with real creative needs.

I build and use Blender tools as part of the creative process. Each add-on focuses on a specific task, with controls that help you get started and room to shape the result around your own scene.

The collection covers audio animation, procedural wildlife, photographed garden plants and stylized water. I also create visual assets and examples to help artists understand how the tools fit into a Blender project.
