Where billboards work — and where they don't

Use them for coverage,
not for every camera distance.

Billboards work well in the middle and far ground, along borders, in flowerbeds, across mass plantings, and in the background. For a single hero plant filling the frame, use a mesh asset instead. Cross 3 and Cross 4 hold up closer than Flat, but they are still designed for volume rather than macro views.

Yes. The photos and textures are licensed for royalty-free use in commercial renders, animation, client projects, and archviz deliveries. The add-on code is GPL because the platform requires that license for Blender add-ons; resale or redistribution of the asset files as a pack is not allowed.

02Will billboards hold up close to camera?Expand

Use billboards for middle- and far-ground vegetation, borders, flowerbeds, and mass planting. Cross 3 and Cross 4 hold closer than Flat, but a full-frame hero plant should be a mesh asset.
